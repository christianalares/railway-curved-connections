# Railway Curved Connections privacy policy

Railway Curved Connections runs on Railway project pages to redraw the architecture graph's visible connection lines. It processes service card positions and connection structure in your browser. It does not read Railway variable values or make requests to the Railway API.

The extension does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information or project data. It stores one setting on your device through Chromium's local extension storage: whether curved connections are enabled. You can change this setting in the extension popup or remove it by uninstalling the extension.

The extension includes a local copy of Viz.js and Graphviz to calculate routes. It does not load executable code from a remote server.

This policy applies only to Railway Curved Connections. Railway's own website has its own data practices. This extension is independent and is not affiliated with Railway.
